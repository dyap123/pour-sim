OpenPour — how to play

Every block is 1 ft³. Build formwork, park a pump, set a pour point, and pour. Concrete falls, flows, and self-levels based on your slump — then takes its initial set and locks.

Engineering model

Form pressure: lateral pressure = unit weight × head of still-plastic concrete. Forms tint green → yellow → red toward their class rating (750–1500 psf); sustained overload bursts them (Blowouts toggle). The advisory next to Start Pour shows max head and rise rate for your weakest form.

Pump pressure: line pressure grows with rate, line length, and stiff mixes, capped by the pump's rated concrete pressure (real Brundage-Bone fleet specs). Over the cap → output derates. Oversized aggregate refuses to pump.

Strength: pick a mix synced from Break Lab and the gain curve is fitted to your actual cylinder breaks; the pour report predicts stripping and design-strength dates.
